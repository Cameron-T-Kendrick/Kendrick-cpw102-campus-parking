# Product Requirements

**Project:** Campus Parking Helper

**Team members:** Cameron Kendrick 

**Date:** 30 September 2026

## User and problem

Who is the user? Students and visitors

What problem does the user have? Drivers have to calculate the cost themselves. 

## Goal

Our program will help the user: Easier for drivers to estimate their parking cost before or during a parking session.

## Requirements

Write three things the program must do. Make each one specific enough to test.

1. The program must be easy for all drivers and vistors to use to input said purchase on parking estimate/sessions via 15 mins intervals.
2. The program must calculate the time to cost for drivers and vistors.
3. The program must easily out put a conformation for the calculation of time to cost ratio. 
 
## Not included

What will this version **not** do?
-Not an actual purchase, just visual the calculation.
-Not to calculate mins/hrs for you and you have to imput it yourself.
-

## Success

We will know the product works when:
-When it out puts said calculation.
-When the timer matches up with said purchase.
-Ease of use.
-Transfer mins to hrs for better visability. Like if its 60 min, then itll say 1 hour or 90 mins, then 1hr 30mins.
